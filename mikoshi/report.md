# Report templates

The Chair writes `verdict.md` in plain English, each point stated once, under about 1,000 words. Seat quotes keep their own voice. The round files hold the detail.

## Decision

```markdown
# Mikoshi: [decision in a few words]

[date] · Seats: [list] · Rounds: [n] · Depth: [full | quick]

## Verdict: [GO | GO WITH CONDITIONS | RESHAPE | WAIT | DON'T DO IT]

**The call:** [one or two sentences to act on, naming the option]

Option scores: [A] x.x · [B] x.x · [C] x.x · Confidence n/10 · Spread n
[Full depth: Support for the resolution x.x/10 · Vote: n endorse, n amend, n dissent]
[Any override applied, in one line]

## Scorecard

| Seat | Weight | Final stance | [A] | [B] | [C] | Pillar score | Moved? | Their line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Math: [one line per option]

## Why the room landed here

[3 to 5 bullets: the arguments that carried it, each tagged with the seat and framework]

## Where they fought

[One short block per matchup or crux: the question, who stood where, who moved and why, how it resolved or why it stayed open]

## Minority report

[The strongest dissent in the seat's words, and what would have to be true for it to be right. When every seat agreed, name the least-tested assumption.]

## Conditions

[Each checkable: a number, a date, or a test result. Mark which must clear before the irreversible step.]

## What to do next

1. This week: [action]
2. [Next milestone with date]
3. [Decision date]

**Cheapest test:** [the smallest thing that checks the riskiest assumption]
**Kill criteria:** [the signal that means stop]

---
Simulated advisors built from each person's material. They are not the people and do not speak for them.
```

## Open question

```markdown
# Mikoshi: [question in a few words]

[date] · Seats: [list] · Rounds: 2

## Where each seat stands

| Seat | Final stance | Confidence | Moved? |
|---|---|---|---|

## Where they agree

[The points every seat accepted, each in one line]

## Where they fought

[One block per matchup or crux: the question, its kind (fact, numbers, or values), who stood where, the best argument on each side, who moved and why]

## What would settle it

[For each open crux, the evidence or test that would decide it]

---
Simulated advisors built from each person's material. They are not the people and do not speak for them.
```

Name a winner in an open question only when the user asked for one.
