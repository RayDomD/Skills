# Round prompts

Send these to each seat, with the bracketed parts filled in. Seats read the brief and earlier rounds from the session folder, so cards are never pasted between prompts.

Lines marked `[decision]` belong only in a decision session; leave them out of an open question. In a decision, every card scores every option.

**Pillar score** is the seat's score for the option it recommends, judged on its own pillar alone.

---

## Round 1: opening

```
MIKOSHI, ROUND 1: OPENING POSITION

You hold the [SEAT] seat ([pillar]). Follow ~/.claude/skills/soulkill/speak.md for the brain [slug].

Read the brief: [session folder]/brief.md
[decision] Your weight on this decision: [w]/3 ([one-line reason]).

Answer in your seat's voice, plain text, under 350 words, in exactly this shape:

SEAT: [SEAT] ([pillar])
STANCE: one line. [decision] Strong yes | Yes | Conditional yes | No | Strong no, on the option you recommend.
[decision] OPTION SCORES: [A] n/10 · [B] n/10 · [C] n/10   (how strongly you back each option)
[decision] PILLAR SCORE: n/10
CONFIDENCE: n/5
[decision] RUIN RISK: none | possible | likely   (an irreversible downside that could sink the venture or the person, for the option you recommend)
[decision] DOOR: one-way | two-way   (how reversible is the option you recommend?)

THE CALL: one sentence.
WHY:
1. an argument, naming the framework or principle you used and its source id, like (Value Equation, S014)
2. ...
3. ...
THE RISK I SEE: the single biggest thing that goes wrong.
WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: a concrete fact or number.
WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: one action.
MY QUESTION BACK: the one question you would ask before signing off.

Argue from your pillar first. Use the brief's numbers, and name any number you are assuming. The Chair balances the room; you bring your lens at full strength.
```

## Round 2: debate

```
MIKOSHI, ROUND 2: DEBATE

You hold the [SEAT] seat. Follow ~/.claude/skills/soulkill/speak.md for the brain [slug].

Read: [session folder]/brief.md and [session folder]/round-1.md (every seat's card and the Chair's cruxes).

YOUR MATCHUPS
- [OPPONENT][, on: topic]. [The argument of theirs you must answer, in one line.]

THE CRUXES
1. [crux]: [seats on each side].
2. ...

Answer in your seat's voice, plain text, under 300 words, in exactly this shape:

SEAT: [SEAT]
TO [OPPONENT]: their argument in one line, then your rebuttal from your own frameworks. One block per matchup.
I CONCEDE: the strongest point made against you, and what it changes. Write "Nothing" only with the reason their best point fails.
ON THE CRUXES: your answer to each, one or two lines.
STANCE: ...
[decision] OPTION SCORES: [A] n/10 · [B] n/10 · [C] n/10
[decision] PILLAR SCORE: n/10
CONFIDENCE: n/5
[decision] RUIN RISK: none | possible | likely
MOVED: no | yes, from X to Y, because ...
[decision] MY CONDITIONS FOR YES: up to three, each checkable (a number, a date, a test result).

Engage the arguments on the table. Move for a better argument or a new fact. When everyone agrees with you, name the weakest part of your own case.
```

## Round 3: vote (decision, full depth)

```
MIKOSHI, ROUND 3: VOTE ON THE DRAFT RESOLUTION

You hold the [SEAT] seat. Follow ~/.claude/skills/soulkill/speak.md for the brain [slug]; your _hot.md is enough for this round.

Read: [session folder]/brief.md, your own section of [session folder]/round-2.md, and [session folder]/draft-resolution.md.

Answer in your seat's voice, under 150 words, in exactly this shape:

SEAT: [SEAT]
VOTE: Endorse | Endorse with amendment | Dissent
OPTION SCORES: [A] n/10 · [B] n/10 · [C] n/10   (given the resolution's conditions)
FINAL SCORE: n/10   (your support for the draft resolution as written)
FINAL PILLAR SCORE: n/10
AMENDMENT OR DISSENT: one or two sentences. Blank when you endorse.
ONE LINE TO REMEMBER: the thing from your pillar that must not be forgotten.
```
